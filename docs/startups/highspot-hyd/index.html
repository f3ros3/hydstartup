Company & Engineering Overview

Highspot Hyderabad is a cornerstone of the Hyderabad technology and startup ecosystem, operating from its modern base in Knowledge City / Raidurg, Hyderabad. Founded in 2012, the company has scaled rapidly to become a recognized powerhouse within the SaaS / Enterprise sector. Enterprise sales enablement unicorn unifying content management, sales coaching, and contextual AI assistants. With a dedicated team of 1,100+ (Hyd: 180+) professionals, Highspot Hyderabad plays a vital role in positioning Hyderabad as India's premier hub for technology and engineering excellence.

Engineering Stack & Architecture

The engineering team at Highspot Hyderabad builds with a modern stack centered around Clojure, Ruby, TypeScript, React, Python, AWS, Elasticsearch. The technical culture prioritizes high-availability microservices architecture, rapid continuous integration and deployment (CI/CD), robust automated testing, and developer autonomy to ship scalable solutions serving global and pan-India enterprises.
