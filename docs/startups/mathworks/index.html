Company & Engineering Overview

MathWorks Hyderabad is a cornerstone of the Hyderabad technology and startup ecosystem, operating from its modern base in Knowledge City / Raidurg, Hyderabad. Founded in 1984, the company has scaled rapidly to become a recognized powerhouse within the SaaS / Enterprise sector. Leading developer of mathematical computing software (MATLAB, Simulink) for engineers, data scientists, and autonomous system builders. With a dedicated team of 800+ (Hyd) professionals, MathWorks Hyderabad plays a vital role in positioning Hyderabad as India's premier hub for technology and engineering excellence.

Engineering Stack & Architecture

The engineering team at MathWorks Hyderabad builds with a modern stack centered around C++, MATLAB, JavaScript, Python, Compilers, Deep Learning. The technical culture prioritizes high-availability microservices architecture, rapid continuous integration and deployment (CI/CD), robust automated testing, and developer autonomy to ship scalable solutions serving global and pan-India enterprises.
