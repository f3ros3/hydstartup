Company & Engineering Overview

Paltech (Palni Inc) is a cornerstone of the Hyderabad technology and startup ecosystem, operating from its modern base in HITEC City, Hyderabad. Founded in 2010, the company has scaled rapidly to become a recognized powerhouse within the SaaS / Enterprise sector. Specialized product engineering partner helping high-growth startups and enterprises build cloud, AI, and data solutions. With a dedicated team of 600+ professionals, Paltech (Palni Inc) plays a vital role in positioning Hyderabad as India's premier hub for technology and engineering excellence.

Engineering Stack & Architecture

The engineering team at Paltech (Palni Inc) builds with a modern stack centered around React, Node.js, Python, AWS, GCP, Microservices, DevOps. The technical culture prioritizes high-availability microservices architecture, rapid continuous integration and deployment (CI/CD), robust automated testing, and developer autonomy to ship scalable solutions serving global and pan-India enterprises.
